Privacy Policy

Faith Mining LLC  ·  Last updated: October 28, 2026  ·  Applies to tools.faithmining.net

In short

Every calculator and converter on this site runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type into a tool is sent to our server, stored by us, or sold to anyone. There are no accounts and no logins. The details below explain the limited cases where any information leaves your device at all.

What we collect

From the tools: nothing. The numbers you enter (hashrate, power use, electricity price, miner price, and similar inputs) are processed on your own device and stay there. Closing the page discards them. We do not receive, store, or see your inputs, and we cannot recover them for you.

By email only: if you write to us, we receive whatever you put in that message, including your email address, so we can reply. We use it only to answer you. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you clearly ask us to.

Automatically, through hosting: like most websites, the hosting service that delivers these pages may record basic technical logs, such as the page requested and the date and time. We do not use those logs to identify you personally.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts or logins, so no usernames or passwords
  • No names, addresses, wallet addresses, or payment details through these tools
  • No storage of calculator inputs, on our servers or anywhere else
  • No sale of personal data to anyone

Cookies

These tools themselves do not set cookies to track you, remember your inputs, or identify you between visits. Your browser may still store cookies set by the third-party services described below, and you can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking them will not stop the calculators from working, although live price and network data (below) may fail to load if those services are blocked.

Third-party services

Google Fonts. The tools load their typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches a font, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as described in Google's own privacy policy. No account or sign-in is involved.

Live market and network data. To show current prices and network difficulty, the tools fetch public data directly from your browser from providers such as CoinGecko (BTC price), Coinbase (BTC price fallback), and Blockchain.info (network difficulty). Those requests go straight from your browser to the provider; we do not proxy them and we do not log them. Each provider has its own privacy policy, and a request to a provider may expose your IP address to that provider, as with any website you visit.

Links to other sites. Tool pages may link to outside sites, including hardware retailers. Some of those links are affiliate links: if you buy through one, Faith Mining LLC may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you, and the retailer will handle that purchase under its own privacy policy.

Advertising: Google AdSense (if ads are shown)

This section applies if and when advertising is shown on this site, now or in the future. It is written to stay accurate whether ads are currently displayed or are added later.

If ads are displayed, they may be served through Google AdSense. In that case, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to this site or other sites. That advertising data is handled by Google under its own policies, not by us, and we do not receive your name or contact details from it.

You can control personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings, and you can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at How Google uses data from sites that use its services. If no ads are displayed, this section has no effect and no advertising cookies are set through this site.

Children's privacy

These tools are not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the tools or their services change. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the tools after an update means you accept the updated policy.
