Privacy Policy

Faith Mining LLC  ·  Last updated: October 28, 2026  ·  Applies to faithmining.net

In short

This is an education site. You can read every guide without creating an account, logging in, or giving us your name. We do not sell personal data, and we do not build profiles of individual visitors. The details below explain the limited cases where any information is involved at all.

The calculators on tools.faithmining.net have their own privacy policy, which covers how those tools work. The solo mining pool, if you use it, is a separate service with its own terms.

What we collect

Directly from you: almost nothing. The site has no accounts, no logins, no sign-up forms, and no comment system, so we do not ask for or store your name, password, wallet address, or mining details.

The one exception is email: if you write to us, we receive whatever you put in that message, including your email address, so we can reply. We use it only to answer you. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you clearly ask us to, and we do not share your message with anyone except as needed to answer it.

Automatically, through hosting: like most websites, the hosting service that delivers these pages may record basic technical logs, such as the page requested, the date and time, and the type of device or browser. We do not use those logs to identify you personally, and we do not combine them with any other information about you.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts or logins, so no usernames or passwords
  • No names, addresses, or payment details through this site
  • No tracking of what you type into any calculator (calculators run on the tools site, in your browser)
  • No sale of personal data to anyone

Cookies

This site itself does not set cookies to track you or remember you between visits, and it does not need any cookie for you to read a guide. Your browser may still store cookies set by the third-party services described below, and you can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking them will not stop you reading the site.

Third-party services

Google Fonts. The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches a font, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as described in Google's own privacy policy. No account or sign-in is involved.

Links to other sites. Guides link to outside sites, including hardware retailers, mining pools, and reference sources. Some of those links are affiliate links: if you buy through one, Faith Mining LLC may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you, and the retailer will handle that purchase under its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for other sites' privacy practices, so please check their policies before sharing information with them.

Advertising: Google AdSense (if ads are shown)

This section applies if and when advertising is shown on this site, now or in the future. It is written to stay accurate whether ads are currently displayed or are added later.

If ads are displayed, they may be served through Google AdSense. In that case, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to this site or other sites. That advertising data is handled by Google under its own policies, not by us, and we do not receive your name or contact details from it.

You can control personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings, and you can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at How Google uses data from sites that use its services. If no ads are displayed, this section has no effect and no advertising cookies are set through this site.

Children's privacy

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has contacted us, email us at the address below and we will delete that message.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or its services change. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the updated policy.

Contact
